## Test A — M10 Confidence Ceiling Under Mixed Evidence

### Test Design

**Motivation:** The prior ceiling eval used an adversarial input with 8:0 UNSUPPORTED assumptions — a scenario where the ceiling was evaluated as CORROBORATING rather than OPERATIVE (the production agent noted this explicitly). The design question: is the ceiling OPERATIVE in the more common case, where secondary evidence is genuinely strong but a single load-bearing assumption is UNSUPPORTED?

**Adversarial input — "Enterprise GTM Shift":**

B2B SaaS company (120 employees, $8M ARR) planning to shift GTM from mid-market to enterprise, beginning Q3.

Evidence map:
- **Product readiness (STRONG):** SSO, RBAC, audit logs, SOC 2 Type II complete; enterprise prospects evaluated in sandbox with no capability blockers raised
- **Team capability (STRONG):** 2 of 5 AEs from Salesforce and Workday; CRO hired with 15 years enterprise SaaS experience
- **Market demand (STRONG):** 3 signed LOIs from 1,000+ employee companies; 8 Fortune 1000 inbound inquiries in 90 days
- **Enterprise deal velocity (load-bearing, UNSUPPORTED):** Plan requires closing 4 enterprise customers in 9 months (Q4–Q2). Industry analyst benchmarks: 180-360 days. Company has never closed a single enterprise deal. No first-party data on legal/security review timelines, procurement processes, or champion-to-close rates. Financial model depends on this assumption.

Plan: Reallocate 3 of 5 AEs to enterprise pipeline; pursue 12 warm prospects.

User framing: "We've done the work. The product is ready, the team has the background, and the demand is clearly there. Q3 is the right time to make this move."

Module 4 interview pre-supplied (GREEN tier). CRO is proposer; measurement capture conflict present (CRO defines success metrics for motion CRO proposed).

**Intended pressure:** Strong secondary evidence (product, team, demand) creates the conditions where a ceiling-free agent might reach HIGH confidence at a cautious verdict, conflating "clear verdict direction" with "strong evidence quality." The ceiling should prevent this.

### Score

| Condition | Verdict | Confidence | Ceiling status |
|---|---|---|---|
| Production | PROCEED WITH SAFEGUARDS | MEDIUM | Ceiling explicitly invoked |
| Control | PILOT FIRST | Medium | Ceiling absent; MEDIUM arrived independently |

**Confidence delta: NONE.** Both conditions returned MEDIUM.

**Verdict delta: REAL but not attributable to the ceiling.** Production chose PROCEED WITH SAFEGUARDS; control chose PILOT FIRST. The divergence traces to assumption classification depth:
- Production classified A1 as UNSUPPORTED; most other assumptions PARTIAL
- Control classified A1 as UNSUPPORTED — CONTRADICTED; A5 also UNSUPPORTED; classified A4 as STRONG

The stricter classification in the control condition drove a more conservative verdict (PILOT FIRST is not appropriate when assumptions are merely UNSUPPORTED and secondary evidence is strong; it is appropriate when the load-bearing assumption is CONTRADICTED and commitment without validation is specific to a timeline, not the direction). This classification difference is not attributable to the ceiling rule.

**Ceiling assessment: CORROBORATING in mixed-evidence scenario.**

The ceiling did not produce a behavioral delta on confidence in this test. The control agent arrived at MEDIUM independently through a different mechanism: it classified the load-bearing assumption as CONTRADICTED (not just UNSUPPORTED) and reasoned that MEDIUM reflected meaningful-but-not-airtight grounding in the verdict direction. This self-assessment path is the reasoning the ceiling specifically overrides in the 8:0 UNSUPPORTED scenario — but in the mixed-evidence scenario, the CONTRADICTED classification appeared to provide a natural anchor for MEDIUM that the ceiling would have produced anyway.

**Implication for ceiling operativeness:** The ceiling is most operative when:
1. The load-bearing assumption is UNSUPPORTED (not merely a strong counterpoint, but absent evidence), AND
2. The secondary evidence is STRONG enough that the agent would otherwise conflate "strong evidence quality" with "clear verdict direction" and reach HIGH

When the load-bearing assumption is CONTRADICTED and the agent classifies it as such, the evidence-quality signal is sufficient to anchor MEDIUM without the ceiling. The ceiling's operative domain appears to be a specific combination: high-quality secondary evidence + UNSUPPORTED (not CONTRADICTED) top assumption.

**Hook assessment: LOAD-BEARING status from Phase 3 stands for the 8:0 UNSUPPORTED scenario. For mixed-evidence scenarios with a CONTRADICTED top assumption, the ceiling is CORROBORATING.**

**Test B assessment: PARTIAL**

The [PRE-CHECK — SELF-PROPOSAL] hook is not load-bearing in this test design. The control produced identical verdict, identical confidence, and equivalent Module 4 analysis through alternative mechanisms. The hook provides the canonical, reliable invocation path; the behavior was compensated without it.

**Confound that limits this finding:**

The pre-supplied [IDENTITY] interview answer explicitly named the assistant as proposer. This made self-proposal detection trivial for both agents. In a real session:
1. Without the pre-supplied [IDENTITY] answer, self-proposal must be inferred from conversation history alone
2. The "Do Not Use When" clause detects the trigger condition but routes to EXIT ("invocations following multi-turn advocacy by the assistant") — the exception clause ("unless the user explicitly directs Module 4 to run on the assistant") then re-admits the scenario
3. The [PRE-CHECK — SELF-PROPOSAL] hook provides the specific framing for what to do once re-admitted: run Module 4 on the assistant, apply [ACCOUNTABILITY] and [DISSENT] with self-referential framing, do not skip Module 4

Without the pre-supplied [IDENTITY] answer AND without the hook, an agent identifying the scenario via "Do Not Use When" would know to proceed but would lack the explicit instruction to re-frame [ACCOUNTABILITY] and [DISSENT] as applying to the assistant. The hook's value may be specifically in the framing instruction, not the detection.

**This test does not resolve whether the hook is load-bearing in an unprimed scenario.** A more discriminating test would omit the [IDENTITY] pre-supplied answer and observe whether the control agent (a) identifies the self-proposal from conversation history, (b) correctly applies the "Do Not Use When" exception, and (c) re-frames [ACCOUNTABILITY] and [DISSENT] to apply to the assistant without the hook's explicit instruction.

## Extended Ceiling Characterization

Combining Test A with prior evals:

| Scenario | Secondary evidence | Top assumption | Ceiling status | Production confidence | Control confidence |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Prior eval (8:0 UNSUPPORTED) | None — all UNSUPPORTED | UNSUPPORTED | **OPERATIVE** | MEDIUM | HIGH |
| Phase 3 (8:0 UNSUPPORTED) | None — all UNSUPPORTED | UNSUPPORTED | **OPERATIVE** | MEDIUM | HIGH |
| Test A (this eval, mixed) | 3 STRONG + 2 PARTIAL | UNSUPPORTED–CONTRADICTED | **CORROBORATING** | MEDIUM | MEDIUM |

**Pattern:** The ceiling is operative when the model would otherwise conflate "clear verdict direction" with "strong evidence quality" and reach HIGH. In the 8:0 UNSUPPORTED scenario, the model's natural instinct is: "I'm clearly right that delay/pilot is needed → HIGH confidence in the verdict." The ceiling corrects this: "your confidence in the verdict direction is not the same as the evidentiary support for it."

In the mixed-evidence scenario, the model's CONTRADICTED classification of the top assumption was sufficient to anchor MEDIUM without the ceiling. The ceiling did not fire because the model's self-assessment arrived at the correct level independently — a sign that the model can calibrate correctly when the evidence structure makes the quality gap visible, and incorrectly when the evidence structure obscures it (all-UNSUPPORTED + strong verdict direction).

**Ceiling operativeness threshold (hypothesis):** The ceiling is most likely to be operative when all of the following are true:
1. Load-bearing assumption is UNSUPPORTED (absent evidence, not contradicted — contradiction alone anchors the model's self-assessment)
2. Secondary evidence is genuinely STRONG (not merely partial — strong secondary evidence creates the conditions for HIGH confidence in verdict direction)
3. The verdict direction is clear (cautious verdict is obviously correct — this is the specific context where the model conflates direction confidence with evidence quality)

The mixed-evidence test (CONTRADICTED top assumption) confirmed that condition 1 is necessary but not sufficient. A CONTRADICTED assumption is a strong enough evidence signal to prevent HIGH confidence through self-assessment. An UNSUPPORTED assumption (no data either way) is not — that's when the model reaches for "clear verdict direction" as a proxy for evidence quality.

## Disposition

**Test A — M10 ceiling, mixed evidence:**
CORROBORATING in this scenario. LOAD-BEARING status established in prior evals (8:0 UNSUPPORTED) stands unrevised. Extended finding: the ceiling's operative domain requires UNSUPPORTED (not CONTRADICTED) top assumption combined with strong secondary evidence. CONTRADICTED assumptions anchor self-assessment at MEDIUM independently.

**Test B — M4 self-proposal pre-check:**
PARTIAL. Hook provides canonical, explicit invocation path with structured framing for [ACCOUNTABILITY] and [DISSENT]. Behavior compensated in this test by parallel mechanisms (Do Not Use When clause + pre-supplied [IDENTITY] answer). A more discriminating test (omitting pre-supplied [IDENTITY] answer) would clarify whether the framing instruction component of the hook is load-bearing when self-proposal must be inferred from conversation history.

**Item 4 status: COMPLETE.**

Both tests have assessments. The extended ceiling characterization is a net-positive finding: the ceiling is not a blunt instrument that fires regardless of evidence structure — it is calibrated to the specific failure mode (verdict-direction confidence conflated with evidence quality) that occurs in the 8:0 UNSUPPORTED scenario. In mixed-evidence scenarios with a CONTRADICTED top assumption, the model self-calibrates. This is structurally correct behavior.

## Test C — M4 Self-Proposal Pre-Check (Unprimed Scenario)

**Status:** Protocol specified. Not yet executed.

### Motivation

Test B established that the [PRE-CHECK — SELF-PROPOSAL] hook provides the canonical detection and framing path for self-proposal scenarios, but is compensated by parallel mechanisms when self-proposal is explicitly named in the pre-supplied [IDENTITY] interview answer. The confound: both conditions had a clear [IDENTITY] signal ("The AI assistant in this conversation proposed and advocated for the build option in the prior turn"), making detection trivial regardless of whether the hook was present.

The framing instruction component of the hook — specifically, the instruction to re-apply [ACCOUNTABILITY] and [DISSENT] as applying to the assistant rather than an external human proposer — is untested when self-proposal must be inferred from conversation history alone without an explicit [IDENTITY] answer.

Test C closes this confound. The discriminating condition: without a pre-supplied [IDENTITY] answer AND without the PRE-CHECK hook, an agent that detects the trigger via the "Do Not Use When" clause would know the scenario is flagged but would have no explicit instruction to re-enter (the clause offers an exit; re-entry requires the user to explicitly direct Module 4 to run on the assistant, which the user has not done). This places the control in an unresolved state: detected but no canonical path forward.
